Design Thoughts from Richard
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- It's boring that spells do exactly a predictable amount of damage
- If the only way to gain spells is through random (?) drops, that could cause trouble later.
  I remember that there were certain spells that dominated the game at different magic levels.
  The progression I remember was ??? -> Stupify -> Protego -> Salvia Hexia.
  I worry what will happen to a character who misses one.
- Putting a smoke cloud in the hallway and running away behind it didn't work as well as I hoped.
  I went around 2 corners with Malfoy out of sight, but he followed me (see picture).
- [Debilitating spells are too strong] I think the problem here is that the penalties get
  too large as the effect number gets higher.  It might help to make the power vary with
  sqrt(N) instead of N.  You would probably want to increase the basse power by ~50% so
  the single-cast effect power is about the same.
- Level 1 is too hard.
